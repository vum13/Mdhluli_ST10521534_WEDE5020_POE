Integrated Access Control & Governance

Restricting unauthorized entry while enabling swift, frictionless movement for authorized personnel is crucial for enterprise security. CSL Technologies designs scalable access control infrastructure suited for corporate headquarters, hotels, industrial facilities, and shopping complexes.

Our Access Control Capabilities

  • Biometric Access Systems: Touchless facial recognition, fingerprint identification, and multi-factor authentication for high-security zones and data centers.
  • RFID Smart Cards & Mobile Credentials: Flexible keycard systems and mobile phone credentials for employees, contractors, and hotel guests.
  • Turnstiles & Vehicle Barriers: Full-height turnstiles, optical speed gates, and automated boom barriers engineered for high-volume entryways and parkades.
  • Visitor Management Integration: Digital check-in kiosks with ID scanning, instant host notifications, and temporary access permissions for corporate guests.

Centralized Management & Time Tracking

Our solutions integrate directly with payroll and HR platforms for precise time and attendance tracking, full audit trails during security incidents, and instant single-click facility lock-downs.
