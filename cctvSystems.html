High-Definition IP CCTV & Video Analytics

Comprehensive video surveillance is essential for maintaining perimeter security, monitoring high-traffic retail areas, and protecting corporate assets. CSL Technologies delivers end-to-end IP CCTV systems designed for seamless integration and continuous monitoring across multi-site operations.

Our CCTV Surveillance Solutions

  • High-Definition IP Camera Systems: Ultra-HD surveillance cameras providing clear visual coverage under any lighting condition, from retail floors to multi-level parking structures.
  • AI-Powered Video Analytics: Automated crowd detection, license plate recognition (ANPR), object tracking, and thermal perimeter protection to proactively detect security threats.
  • Centralized Command Centers: Scalable Network Video Recorders (NVR) and Video Management Software (VMS) configured for real-time monitoring and off-site control rooms.
  • Remote Mobile & Desktop Access: Encrypted remote viewing capabilities for executive teams and operational staff to inspect security feeds anytime, anywhere.

System Upgrades & Maintenance Agreements

We provide full hardware migrations—upgrading legacy analog camera setups to modern IP networks—alongside customized Service Level Agreements (SLAs) for routine lens cleaning, storage auditing, and preventative maintenance.
