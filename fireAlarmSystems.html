Fire Protection & Detection Systems

Early detection and reliable containment are vital for protecting multi-story commercial buildings, shopping malls, hotels, and corporate enterprises. CSL Technologies designs, installs, and maintains certified fire safety infrastructure that complies with strict national regulations[cite: 1].

Our Fire System Capabilities

  • Conventional & Addressable Fire Alarm Systems: Tailored detection panels that pinpoint the exact location of a smoke or heat trigger across massive facilities.
  • Early Warning Smoke Detection (Aspirating Systems): High-sensitivity smoke detection designed for server rooms, data centers, and specialized industrial spaces.
  • S.A.Q.C.C Compliant Installations: Fully certified installations backed by professional maintenance and compliance testing.
  • Integrated Emergency Evacuation: Automated voice evacuation systems and emergency lighting integration to guide occupants safely during critical incidents.
